What We Do

Project Consultants

Project consultants act as specialized advisors to manage risk, ensure quality, and streamline the development lifecycle.

01

Feasibility & Market Studies

Analyzing site potential, performing financial underwriting, and conducting market research to assess demand.

02

Regulatory Compliance & Permitting

Navigating local, state, and federal regulations, managing zoning laws, and obtaining necessary approvals.

03

Budget & Financial Management

Establishing project budgets, controlling costs, securing funding, and approving payment certificates.

04

Design & Team Coordination

Coordinating with architects, engineers, and specialists to develop site plans and project specifications.

05

Procurement & Vendor Management

Managing the bidding process to select contractors and managing vendor contracts.

06

Risk Management

Identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks related to budget, schedule, and construction execution.

Development

Estate Developers

Estate developers, or property developers, are the entities that coordinate all activities, from purchasing land to selling the finished product.

01

Site Selection & Acquisition

Identifying and purchasing land or buildings with development potential.

02

Development Planning

Creating a vision, defining the project scope, and planning the layout and design.

03

Securing Financing

Arranging construction loans, equity financing, and managing capital structures.

04

Construction Management

Overseeing the actual construction process to convert the site into an operating asset.

05

Marketing & Leasing

Executing marketing strategies and managing lease administration for residential or commercial units.

06

Property Management

Maintaining the quality of residential or commercial holdings after project completion.
